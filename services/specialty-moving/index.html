How we do it

The parts of this service that actually decide whether it goes well.

Weight decides the crew, not volume

A gun safe is small and can weigh seven hundred pounds. A treadmill is awkward and dense. Our calculator adds a mover automatically when heavy items are on the list, because sending three people to do a four-person lift is how backs and staircases get broken.

Crated, not wrapped

Marble and glass tops, large framed art, chandeliers and mirrors get a crate built to their dimensions. Blankets are not protection for something rigid and brittle — the load shifts, and rigid things crack.

Condition recorded before and after

We photograph specialty pieces at pickup and at delivery. If something does go wrong, there is a record instead of a disagreement, and claims close in days rather than turning into a dispute.

Stairs change everything

An upright piano on a straight ground-floor carry and the same piano down two flights with a turn are different jobs with different crews and different equipment. Tell us about the stairs when you book and the quote reflects reality.

Questions we get every week

What does it cost to move a piano?

It depends on the type and the access. An upright on a ground floor is very different from a baby grand down a flight of stairs. Piano handling is a separate line on the quote so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

Can you move a gun safe down stairs?

Usually, but not always. It depends on the weight, the staircase and the landing. We ask for the make and weight before quoting and will tell you honestly if it needs specialist rigging rather than a moving crew.

Do you move pool tables?

Yes, including disassembly and reassembly. Slate is heavy and brittle and has to come apart. Releveling after reassembly is done by a table technician, which we can coordinate.

How do you handle artwork?

Smaller pieces go in picture cartons. Anything large, valuable or irreplaceable gets a custom crate. For high-value collections we recommend full value protection and an appraisal on file.

Will you move a fish tank or a wine collection?

Fish tanks empty only. Wine we move, and for a serious cellar we will talk through temperature exposure and timing before agreeing a date.
