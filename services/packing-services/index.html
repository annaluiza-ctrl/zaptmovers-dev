How we do it

The parts of this service that actually decide whether it goes well.

Three levels, and most people need the middle one

Full pack means we do everything. Fragile-only means we handle the kitchen, glass and art while you do clothes and books. Partial is whatever mix you want. Most customers pack their own bedrooms and hand us the kitchen, which is exactly the right instinct.

The kitchen is the real work

A kitchen is the slowest room in any house and the one with the most breakables. Dish packs are double-walled for a reason, plates go in on edge rather than stacked flat, and glasses get individually wrapped. A crew does a full kitchen in an afternoon. It takes most people three evenings.

Some things cannot be boxed, so we build for them

Marble tops, glass tabletops, large mirrors, framed art, chandeliers. These get custom crates or mirror cartons cut to size. It is the single best money you can spend on a move, because these are the items with no replacement.

Unpacking is a service too

We can unpack into cabinets and closets and take every box, sheet of paper and strip of tape away with us the same day. Most people never book it and then spend a month with a wall of flattened cardboard in the garage.

Questions we get every week

How long does packing take?

A two-bedroom takes a crew most of a day. A four-bedroom house is usually two days. We normally pack the day before the move, so load day starts with everything already boxed.

Do you supply the boxes?

Yes, and they are included in a full or partial pack. If you are packing yourself, you can order materials from us and we deliver them before move day.

Can I pack some rooms and you do the rest?

That is the most common arrangement. Just be honest about what you will actually finish. Half-packed rooms on move morning cost more than booking the pack would have.

Is there anything you will not pack?

Hazardous materials, perishables, and anything irreplaceable that should travel with you — passports, deeds, jewelry, medication.

Should I leave clothes on hangers?

Yes. We bring wardrobe boxes on move day and clothes go straight in on the hanger, then straight into the new closet. Do not fold a closet into boxes.
